Account & data

Delete your Lit Chain account

Request deletion in the app

Sign in to Lit Chain, open Library, choose Edit Profile, and select Delete Account. Confirm the request to submit it and sign out.

Request deletion without the app

If you cannot access the app, email info@litchain.org from the email address associated with your Lit Chain account and state that you want the account deleted. We may ask you to verify account ownership before processing the request.

What is deleted

We process deletion of account information and associated personal data that is no longer required to operate the service or meet legal, security, fraud-prevention, or recordkeeping obligations.

Community content

Content that forms part of a shared discussion or reading chain may be retained where necessary to preserve the continuity of that discussion. When retained after account deletion, identifying profile information will be removed or anonymized during deletion processing.

Contact info@litchain.org.
